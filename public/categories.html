Market Categories

Markets for every interest

Prediction markets span far more than politics. Playmatez offers markets across ten categories — from crypto and stocks to weather, sports, and esports. Each one names its resolution source before it opens, so you always know what determines the outcome.

How categories work

Resolution source is named first

Every market states the public source that will determine its outcome — an official result, a published index, a named data feed — before anyone takes a position.

Markets close before the event

Trading locks before the event starts, so the crowd's expectation is captured in advance — not changed mid-event. The final price reflects what participants collectively believed.

Price is probability, not certainty

A 70 cent share implies a 70% chance — which means there's still a 30% chance it doesn't happen. Understanding that distinction is the single most important habit for newcomers.
